SEO CONTENT UPDATE 2024

27 Mar 2024 · By SMAC Digital

All posts or Get a free consultation

One of the most important things you can do to maintain your website's rating and attract organic visitors is to improve its content. Google and other search engines are always tweaking their algorithms to provide relevant and appropriate results to their users. This is why it's so important for digital marketers at the best digital marketing agency to always provide fresh, original content updates. Search engines place importance on original content since it is so difficult to maintain content's originality in the vast information universe.

Google is coming up with a core update in March 2024 to further optimize search result quality. So, let us see what exactly the update is from Google. 

The Google Core upgrade—what is it?

  • Google Core Updates are modifications to the search algorithm that increase the relevancy and quality of search results. Search engine rankings and traffic are likely to change as a result of algorithm updates; some sites may see an improvement in ranking, while others may see a decline.
     
  • Google typically releases core upgrades several times a year. SEO or search engine optimisation, experts track how well their websites are doing and make changes as needed. Google keeps updates to its algorithms under wraps. Professionals should be responsible for doing analytics and adjusting their SEO methods as needed.

What is the objective of the Google Core update?

  • Google Core Updates work to enhance search results by making sure that top-ranking sites match users' search criteria and by improving Google's understanding of search queries and content.
  • They exclude manipulative or low-quality websites and give preference to those with superior content, originality, and expertise. To keep up with current trends, they are improving their algorithms to identify and rank relevant information.
  • Google must ensure that the information it provides to its consumers comes from trustworthy sources. By implementing these changes, they want to discourage unfair practices, level up competition, and boost the exposure of worthy websites in SERPs. 

What is the March 2024 core update from Google?

Google released more than ten core updates in 2023. Similarly, with March 2024 core being the first update of 2024, Google may release a slew of upgrades throughout the year. To increase a website's ranking, SEO experts use a variety of strategies. This may be genuine or not. 

Google has improved its algorithm in the March 2024 core update to decrease deceitful and unreliable stuff from search results and present people with completely authentic information.

The March 2024 core update is going to focus on improved quality ranking and spam policies and it will filter the content into three types of categories from SERPs. 

Scaled content abuse

The term "scaled content abuse" refers to the fraudulent practice of filling search engines with low-quality or copied information to deceive the system and make the website rank higher. These days, automated content generation tools are in abundance and people misuse these tools for automated procedures including automated content generation, content farming, keyword stuffing, and content scraping. 

It is considered a black-hat SEO tactic, violates search engine guidelines, and decreases the credibility of the search engine. Google has now implemented some tweaks in the March 2024 core update, building on previous updates, to reduce the prevalence of imitated content.

Site reputation abuse

Abuse of site reputation is a dishonest strategy to boost a website's ranking. It includes the purchase or sale of backlinks, engagement in link schemes, manipulation of guest blogging, and several other activities. SEO professionals collaborate with higher-ranked, authoritative websites to establish backlinks. Those connections are entirely irrelevant to the users of the hosting website.

Google will mark these manipulative backlinks as low-value content and spam with the March 2024 core update.

Expired domain abuse

The dishonest practice of obtaining expired domain names and utilizing them for illicit and misleading purposes is known as expired domain abuse. This is a scam where scammers wait for domains to expire before attempting to register them right away.

Users may not know about the ownership change of the website and they will see irrelevant content. This is just like selling fake products in the name of a known brand. The March 2024 core update will mark all these websites as spam.

Why is it important for SEO professionals to understand and prepare for the Google Core update?

  • Google Core updates significantly impact website rankings worldwide. Website owners should anticipate changes in search visibility and take proactive steps to preserve or enhance their ranks by recognising these modifications.
  • Staying up-to-date with Google's core upgrades will help you enhance the user experience and organic traffic. Being ready for core changes will help you keep your organic traffic constant and reduce the risk of losing guests.
  • If you want to rank higher in search results than your competitors, you need to keep up with Google's algorithm updates and implement them faster. Keep yourself informed about Google Core Updates and be prepared for them if you want your SEO efforts to succeed in the long term.

Timeline 

The Google March 2024 core update is going to be effective on May 5, 2024. This will give SEO professionals plenty of time to adjust their SEO strategies with the updates and eliminate potential losses.

Summary

The goal of Google's core update, scheduled for March 2024, is to enhance the user experience by decreasing the amount of deceptive and fake content. By classifying content as low-value, abusive of site reputation, or abusive of expired domains, it will concentrate on quality ranking and spam regulations. Since this upgrade will have a major effect on website rankings worldwide, SEO experts need to get ready for it.
