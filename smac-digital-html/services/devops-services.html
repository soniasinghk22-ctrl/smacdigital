Overview

What this service covers

DevOps is a combination of cultural philosophies, practices, and tools that increases an organization’s ability to deliver applications and services at high velocity.

SMAC Digital offers robust DevOps services tailored to automate and streamline your development and deployment pipeline.

01

DevOps Strategy & Planning

We begin by understanding your infrastructure and delivery pipeline. Our strategy focuses on faster delivery, reduced failure rates, and automated deployment cycles. We work with your team to define goals, select tools, and create roadmaps that align with your business objectives.

  • Assessment of current IT setup
  • Goal setting and KPIs
  • Roadmap creation and timeline planning

02

CI/CD Pipeline Automation

Automated CI/CD pipelines ensure that your code is tested and deployed quickly and consistently. We implement modern tools such as Jenkins, GitHub Actions, and GitLab CI to enable seamless code integration and faster delivery cycles.

  • Continuous Integration with automated testing
  • Deployment automation to staging and production
  • Rollback and version control mechanisms

03

Containerization & Orchestration

Leverage containers to build lightweight, portable, and consistent environments for development and production. SMAC Digital uses Docker for containerization and Kubernetes for orchestration, enabling easy scaling and high availability.

  • Docker-based containerization
  • Kubernetes cluster setup and management
  • Helm chart deployments and auto-scaling

04

Monitoring & Logging

Our monitoring services ensure real-time visibility into your application performance and infrastructure health. We integrate tools like Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK Stack to deliver accurate insights for proactive decision-making.

  • Real-time application monitoring
  • Log aggregation and visualization
  • Alerting and incident management

05

DevOps Tools We Use

  • CI/CD: Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, CircleCI
  • Containers: Docker, Podman
  • Orchestration: Kubernetes, OpenShift
  • IaC: Terraform, Ansible, CloudFormation
  • Monitoring: Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog
  • Security: SonarQube, Snyk, Vault
  • Cloud: AWS, Azure, GCP
